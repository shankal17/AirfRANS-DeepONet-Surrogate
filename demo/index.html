NACA Airfoil Neural Surrogate Demo

Pick a NACA 4- or 5-digit airfoil and flow conditions. In real time, the surrogate predicts the pressure coefficient Cp and skin-friction coefficient Cf at 800 surface points, then integrates them into the lift and drag coefficients Cl and Cd. This particular problem of a 2d airfoil has other acceptably fast solutions, but neural operators extend into other more difficult applications where other fast solutions do not exist.
